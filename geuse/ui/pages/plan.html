Session guidelines

Always warm up for 2 minutes before starting — gentle hand shakes and wrist rolls are enough.
Stop immediately if pain exceeds 7/10. Some mild discomfort during movement is expected — sharp or sudden pain is not.
Rest at least one full day between sessions — recovery is when the real progress happens.
Consistency matters more than intensity. Showing up regularly at low effort beats occasional hard sessions every time.
